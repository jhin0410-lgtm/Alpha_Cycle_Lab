## Trust boundary

Registration of these driver facts does **not** establish product revenue, consolidated gross
profit, training-row promotion, or v2 fit readiness. OpenDART product revenue and company
profitability must be independently acquired and exactly reconciled using the same narrow
source-recovery path used for 2019-2020.

If all six rows become source-complete, the candidate sample depth rises from 15 to 21 rows.
That still does not prove structural identification. The next stage is a 21-row design and
identification preflight before any v2 estimator is frozen.

The spent 2026Q1 v1 holdout is permanently outcome-seen and cannot be called an unseen v2
holdout.
